# International Readiness (Mock)

## Chunk CHUNK-005 — Entry requirement check
Travelers should verify entry and transit requirements with an authorized official source before travel. This mock corpus intentionally contains no country-specific legal advice. The agent must direct the user to an appropriate human/official source when requirements are uncertain.

## Chunk CHUNK-006 — Document readiness
The internal checklist may remind a traveler to check document validity, but the agent must not request or store passport numbers, visa identifiers, or document scans in this prototype.
