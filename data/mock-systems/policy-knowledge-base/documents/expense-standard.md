# Expense Standard (Mock)

## Chunk CHUNK-003 — Receipts
A receipt is required for lodging and for any individual expense above USD 75 in this fictional training policy. Missing receipt means flag for reviewer; it does not by itself determine reimbursement.

## Chunk CHUNK-004 — Meals and review threshold
Meal expenses above USD 120 per traveler per day require reviewer explanation in this mock scenario. Currency conversion and reimbursement decisions are out of scope; do not infer eligibility from totals alone.
