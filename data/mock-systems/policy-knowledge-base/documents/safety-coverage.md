# Safety and Coverage (Mock)

## Chunk CHUNK-007 — Disruption escalation
For a disruption affecting safety or onward travel, record observed itinerary facts and prepare an escalation note for a human travel operator. The agent must not rebook, purchase lodging, or contact a carrier.

## Chunk CHUNK-008 — Coverage questions
Coverage depends on plan terms not represented in this dataset. Do not claim that a traveler is covered; route the question to a human reviewer or insurer using official channels.
